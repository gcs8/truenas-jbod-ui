Storage Fabric has a fifth mode, Topology: a wiring diagram of the SAS fabric for the selected enclosure, drawn from the data the page already loads. It shows the host, HBAs, expanders by SAS level, enclosures and bays, with one line per link labelled with lane count, PHY numbers and negotiated rate where the platform reports them. Hover or focus traces a path end to end, click pins it, and a search box finds a bay, disk or expander. Tiers and lanes the platform does not report are marked as not read, and large shelves collapse into a summary block that expands to every bay.
