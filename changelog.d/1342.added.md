Storage Fabric has a fifth mode, Topology: a wiring diagram of the SAS fabric for the selected enclosure, drawn from the data the page already loads. It shows the host, HBAs, expanders by SAS level, enclosures and bays, with one line per link labelled with lane count, PHY numbers and negotiated rate where the platform reports them; each HBA's lines have their own colour, and every bay shows one mark per path, different in shape for each path state. Hover or keyboard focus traces a path end to end, click pins it and fills the inspector, and a search box finds a bay, disk, serial, SAS address or expander. The view uses the full page width, keeps its scroll position while you select, and has a Fit control that says when fitted text would be smaller than 12 px. Tiers, lanes and completeness the platform does not report are marked as not reported, disks without a known physical location are never drawn as bays, records the view cannot draw are named on the page, and large shelves collapse into a summary block that expands to every bay.
