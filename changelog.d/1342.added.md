Storage Fabric has a fifth mode, Topology: a wiring diagram of the SAS fabric for the selected enclosure, drawn from the data the page already loads. It shows the host, HBAs, expanders by SAS level, enclosures and bays, with one line per link labelled with lane count, PHY numbers and negotiated rate where the platform reports them; each parent sits over the items it feeds, each HBA's lines have their own colour and line pattern, where two lines cross one of them has a small gap, and every bay shows one mark per path, different in shape for each path state, with member health never drawn as a path state. Hover or keyboard focus traces a path end to end, click pins it and fills the inspector, Esc clears both, and a search box finds a bay by the number the chassis prints, a disk, serial, SAS address or expander. The view uses the full page width, opens with the host and HBAs in view, says when the drawing is wider than its box, keeps its scroll position while you select, and has a Fit control that says when fitted text would be smaller than 12 px. Tiers, lanes and completeness the platform does not report are marked as not reported, an unknown or conflicting fabric kind is named without a claim about what was collected, disks without a known physical location are never drawn as bays, node, link and trace records the view cannot draw (unsupported kinds, missing ends, duplicates, links that point up the tiers) are named on the page, and large shelves collapse into a summary block that expands to every bay.
