Backups: a remote copy's upload check is now read from the provider recorded with that copy at upload, so changing a target's provider no longer presents an older upload as read back; a remote copy made by an earlier version and recorded as read back reads as "provider checksum or read-back" until its own Verify passes. A saved Verify result that reports success beside an outcome other than matched, or beside a malformed outcome, is no longer shown as Verified or as a check that worked, in the list or the details.
