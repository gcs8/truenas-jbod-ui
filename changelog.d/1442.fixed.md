Verify and download of a remote backup copy now reclaim earlier fetch workspaces whose clean-up failed, once they are shown to belong to the scheduler account, to be unused, and to hold only the fetched file and its fetch's marker. Anything else is left in place and counted in the scheduler log, and while four or more such workspaces are left a remote fetch is refused with the count instead of keeping another full copy. Remote fetches are also refused when the scheduler state folder is a link, belongs to another account or is writable by others.
