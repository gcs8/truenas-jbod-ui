CORE controller device lists: a `show devices` row whose speed mprutil prints as `Unk` or `???` is now read with its own enclosure handle and slot instead of taking them from the columns after it, so the device is counted in its own enclosure. A row whose columns after the device type do not read as speed, enclosure handle and slot now leaves the device list not read whole and withholds the enclosure's device count, instead of placing the device under another enclosure handle.
