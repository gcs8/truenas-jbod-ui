The BMC web inventory now reads up to 64 controllers and 4,096 drives per
controller, and history requests admit up to 256 enclosure scopes and 16,384
bays, so a full 1,024-disk expander is read instead of refused. A reply or
request over a limit is still refused, and the message names the requested
count beside the limit; the row and byte budgets on history reads are
unchanged.
