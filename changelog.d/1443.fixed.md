SES over SSH: an sg_ses page with more elements or text than the parser accepts (8,192 elements or 4 MiB per page) is no longer dropped as if the device had no enclosure. The page is still refused, and the refresh now shows a warning naming the page, the SES device, the host, its element or character count and the limit, so missing bays from that page are explained; an identify-light readback from such a page is reported as unknown.
